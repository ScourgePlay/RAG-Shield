ACME Corporation — Annual Leave and Holiday Policy

Full-time employees accrue an annual leave entitlement of 25 working days per calendar year, exclusive of public holidays. Entitlement accrues monthly from the date of joining and is pro-rated for part-time staff according to contracted hours.

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken before 31 March or they are forfeited. Carry-over beyond this limit requires written approval from the Head of People.

Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the intended start date. Requests submitted with less notice may be refused at the discretion of the line manager, except in cases of bereavement or medical emergency.

A leave blackout period operates from 15 December to 5 January inclusive, during which annual leave is granted only where business cover can be demonstrated. Teams providing customer-facing cover are subject to an additional blackout during the final week of each quarter.

On termination of employment, accrued but untaken annual leave is paid as a pro-rata settlement in the final salary payment. Leave taken in excess of accrued entitlement is recovered from the final payment at the employee's daily rate.

Annual leave is approved by the employee's direct line manager. Absences exceeding 10 consecutive working days additionally require countersignature from the relevant department head before the request is confirmed.
