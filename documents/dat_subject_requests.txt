ACME Corporation — Data Subject Requests and Deletion

A data subject access request must receive a substantive response within 30 calendar days of receipt. The clock starts on the date the request is received, not the date it is assigned.

Where a request is complex or involves multiple systems, the response period may be extended by a further 60 days. The data subject must be informed of the extension and its reason within the original 30-day window.

The identity of the requester must be verified before any personal data is disclosed. Verification uses two independent identifiers and is recorded against the request file.

No fee is charged for a first data subject access request. A reasonable administrative fee may be applied to repeat requests from the same individual within a 6-month period.

Approved deletion requests are executed within 30 days of approval across all production systems and backups scheduled for the next rotation. A deletion certificate is issued to the requester.

Any request that cannot be fulfilled within the standard process is escalated to the Data Protection Officer for decision. The Officer's determination is recorded with reasons in the request file.
