ACME Corporation — Building Access and Security

An access badge must be worn visibly at all times within company premises. Staff are expected to challenge politely, or report, any individual on site without a visible badge.

Visitors must be signed in at reception and escorted by their host at all times while on site. Visitors are issued a temporary badge that expires automatically at the end of the day.

Standard building access hours are 06:00 to 22:00, Monday to Friday. Badge access outside these hours is declined unless out-of-hours permission has been granted.

Out-of-hours and weekend access requires written approval from a department head, recorded in the access management system. Approval is granted for a defined period and expires automatically.

A lost or stolen access badge must be reported to the facilities team within 24 hours. The badge is deactivated on report and a replacement issued on verification of identity.

Tailgating, meaning allowing another person to follow through a controlled door on a single badge presentation, is prohibited. Each person must present their own badge at every controlled entry point.
