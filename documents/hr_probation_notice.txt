ACME Corporation — Probation and Notice Periods

New employees serve a standard probationary period of 6 months from their start date. During probation, performance is reviewed informally each month and formally at the midpoint and conclusion of the period.

A probationary period may be extended once by a maximum of 3 months where performance concerns have been documented and a development plan is in place. Further extension is not permitted.

During the probationary period, either party may terminate the employment relationship by giving 1 week of written notice. No reason is required, though the company will ordinarily set out its rationale in writing.

Following confirmation of appointment, the standard notice period is 1 calendar month for both the employee and the company. Notice must be given in writing and takes effect from the date of receipt.

Employees at senior management grade and above are subject to a 3-month notice period in both directions. This is specified in the individual contract of employment and supersedes the standard arrangement.

The company reserves the right to place an employee on garden leave for all or part of the notice period. Salary and contractual benefits continue during garden leave, but the employee may not attend the workplace or contact clients.
