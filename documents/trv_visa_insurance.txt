ACME Corporation — Visa, Insurance and Traveller Safety

Corporate travel insurance covers every employee automatically on business travel, with no individual registration required. Cover includes medical treatment, repatriation and trip disruption.

Visa application fees, associated appointment costs and expedited processing charges are reimbursed in full where the visa is required for business travel. Receipts must be submitted with the claim.

Travel to destinations on the high-risk schedule requires written clearance from the corporate security team before booking. Clearance considers current advisories and the business necessity of the trip.

Travellers must register an emergency contact and their itinerary in the traveller tracking system before departure. The record enables location-based notification during a disruption or security event.

Vaccinations and antimalarial medication required for a business destination are paid for by the company. Employees arrange appointments through the occupational health provider and claim the cost as an expense.

A 24-hour travel assistance line is available to all travellers for medical, security and logistical emergencies while abroad. The number is printed on the corporate travel card issued to every traveller.
