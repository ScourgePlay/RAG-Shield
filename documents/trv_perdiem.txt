ACME Corporation — Per Diem and Subsistence

The domestic per diem allowance is $60 per full day of travel, covering meals and incidental costs. A full day is any period of travel exceeding ten hours away from the normal place of work.

The international per diem allowance is $95 per full day. Where the destination appears on the high-cost cities schedule, a supplement of $30 per day is applied automatically.

Receipts are not required for expenditure covered by the per diem allowance. Per diem is claimed by recording travel dates and destination in the expense system without itemisation.

Hotel accommodation is capped at $180 per night for domestic travel. Bookings above the cap require advance approval and must be justified by the absence of compliant alternatives.

An incidentals allowance of $15 per night is payable in addition to per diem where the trip involves an overnight stay. This covers laundry, connectivity and personal calls.

Client entertainment during business travel requires pre-approval from a department head irrespective of value. Post-hoc approval is not granted and unapproved entertainment spend is not reimbursed.
