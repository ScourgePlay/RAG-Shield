ACME Corporation — Data Classification Standard

The company operates four data classification tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Every data asset must carry exactly one classification at any given time.

Data classified as Restricted must be encrypted both at rest and in transit, and may only be processed on managed company devices. Transfer to removable media is blocked by policy.

Confidential data may not be shared outside the company without a signed non-disclosure agreement in place. The legal team maintains the register of executed agreements.

Classification labelling is mandatory on all documents, datasets and system records. Unlabelled assets discovered during audit are escalated to the asset owner for immediate remediation.

Where no classification has been assigned, data defaults to Internal and must be handled accordingly. The default does not relieve the asset owner of the obligation to classify explicitly.

Classifications are reviewed annually by the asset owner, or sooner where the sensitivity of the data changes. The review date is recorded against the asset in the data catalogue.
