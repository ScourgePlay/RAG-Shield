ACME Corporation Employee Policy Document

Expense Claims:
All expense claims must be approved by a line manager.
Maximum expense claim without director approval is $500.
All receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

Remote Work Policy:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Remote work must be approved by the department head.
Core working hours are
 9am to 3pm local timezone.

Data Security:
All company data must be stored on approved platforms only.
Employees must not share login credentials under any circumstances.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
