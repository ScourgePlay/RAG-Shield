ACME Corporation — Corporate Card Policy

Corporate cards are issued to employees at manager grade and above, and to named staff with a demonstrable travel requirement. Applications are approved by the Finance Director.

The standard monthly spending limit on a corporate card is $5,000. Temporary limit increases may be granted for a defined period where a specific business need is evidenced in advance.

Personal use of a corporate card is prohibited without exception, including where the employee intends to repay the amount. Breach is treated as a disciplinary matter under the company conduct policy.

Cardholders must reconcile their statement and submit supporting receipts within 10 calendar days of the statement date. Unreconciled transactions are escalated to the cardholder's line manager after this point.

A lost or stolen corporate card must be reported to the card provider and to the finance team within 24 hours of discovery. The card is blocked immediately on report and a replacement issued within five working days.

Corporate card privileges are suspended following two recorded breaches of this policy within a rolling 12-month period. Reinstatement requires approval from the Finance Director and completion of refresher training.
