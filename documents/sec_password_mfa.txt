ACME Corporation — Password and Authentication Policy

Account passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters in length. Complexity is achieved through length and unpredictability rather than mandatory character class mixing, in line with current guidance.

Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system issues reminders at 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry, and access is suspended where a password is not changed by the expiry date.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access to company systems, including VPN, webmail and cloud administration consoles. Access attempts without a second factor are rejected.

All staff must store work credentials in the company-provided password manager. Storing credentials in browsers, spreadsheets, notes applications or physical documents is prohibited.

A new password may not match any of the previous 12 passwords used on the account. The system enforces this automatically and rejects reuse at the point of change.

An account is locked after 5 consecutive failed authentication attempts. Lockout persists for 30 minutes or until released by the service desk following identity verification.
