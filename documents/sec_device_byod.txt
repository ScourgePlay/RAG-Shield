ACME Corporation — Device Security and BYOD

Full-disk encryption is mandatory on every device used to access company data, including laptops, desktops and removable storage. Devices failing an encryption check are blocked from the network.

Personal devices used for work must be enrolled in the company mobile device management platform before access is granted. Enrolment applies a security baseline and enables remote wipe of the work container only.

Operating system and security patches must be applied within 14 days of release. Devices more than 14 days behind on critical patches are placed in a restricted network segment until remediated.

Jailbroken, rooted or otherwise modified devices may not be used to access company data under any circumstances. Detection results in immediate revocation of access pending investigation.

On termination of employment, company data is remotely wiped from all enrolled devices on the final working day. For personal devices, only the managed work container is removed.

Devices must apply an automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity, requiring re-authentication to resume. This setting is enforced by policy and cannot be altered by the user.
