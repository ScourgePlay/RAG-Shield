ACME Corporation — Workspace and Health and Safety

Desks must be booked at least 24 hours in advance through the workspace booking system. Unbooked desks may be released to other staff from 10:00 on the day.

Hot-desking operates in zones B and C. Zone A retains allocated seating for teams requiring fixed equipment or handling Restricted data.

Fire evacuation drills are conducted quarterly at each site. Participation is mandatory and attendance is recorded against the staff roll at the assembly point.

A minimum of 2 trained first aiders must be present on each occupied floor during working hours. Names and locations are displayed at every floor entrance and lift lobby.

All workplace accidents and near misses must be recorded in the incident log, regardless of whether injury occurred. Entries are reviewed monthly by the health and safety committee.

Food and drink other than water are prohibited in laboratory and server areas. Designated break areas are provided on each floor for consumption of food and hot drinks.
