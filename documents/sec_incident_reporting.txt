ACME Corporation — Security Incident Reporting

A suspected security incident must be reported within 1 hour of discovery. Reporting is required even where the reporter is uncertain whether an incident has genuinely occurred.

Incidents are reported to the security team by email to the security reporting address or by the 24-hour incident hotline. Both channels are monitored continuously, including outside business hours.

Incidents are classified on a four-level severity scale from P1, denoting critical business impact or confirmed data loss, through to P4, denoting minor issues with no material impact.

A P1 incident receives an initial response within 30 minutes of being raised, at any hour. The on-call security engineer acknowledges the incident and convenes the response bridge.

A post-incident review is completed within 5 working days of incident closure. The review documents root cause, timeline, and remedial actions with named owners and target dates.

Employees must not disclose details of a security incident outside the company, including on social media, until the communications team has issued formal guidance. Enquiries from press are referred to the communications team.
