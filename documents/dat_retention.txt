ACME Corporation — Data Retention Schedule

Payroll records are retained for 7 years from the end of the tax year to which they relate. Retention is required for statutory audit and is not subject to early deletion requests.

Customer records are retained for 6 years after the end of the customer relationship. At the end of this period records are deleted automatically unless subject to a live legal hold.

CCTV footage is retained for 30 days and then overwritten automatically. Footage required for an investigation must be extracted and preserved within the retention window.

Corporate email is retained for 3 years from the date of sending or receipt. Mailboxes of departed employees are preserved for the remainder of the retention period and then deleted.

Recruitment records for unsuccessful candidates are retained for 12 months from the date of the hiring decision. Candidates may request earlier deletion at any point during this period.

System and application logs are retained for 13 months to support security investigation and trend analysis. Logs are stored in an append-only store and cannot be edited after write.
