ACME Corporation — Equipment Allocation and Return

Company laptops are refreshed on a 3-year cycle. Replacement outside the cycle is available where a device fails or where a documented technical requirement cannot be met by the existing specification.

Each workstation is allocated a maximum of 2 monitors as standard. Additional displays require a documented business justification approved by the department head and the IT team.

A one-off home office allowance of $300 is available to employees working remotely on a regular basis. The allowance covers desk, chair and lighting and is claimed through the expense system.

An ergonomic workstation assessment is available to any employee on request, conducted by the occupational health provider. Recommended equipment arising from an assessment is provided at company cost.

All company equipment must be returned within 5 working days of the final day of employment. Return is arranged through the facilities team, which issues a signed receipt for each item.

Where equipment is damaged through demonstrable negligence, an excess of $100 is recharged to the employee. Accidental damage and fair wear and tear are not recharged.
