ACME Corporation — Procurement Thresholds and Authority

Purchases with a total value below $1,000 require a single written quotation. The requesting department retains the quotation on file for audit purposes for a period of six years.

Purchases valued between $1,000 and $10,000 require three independent written quotations. Where three quotations cannot reasonably be obtained, a single-source justification must be approved by the Finance Director.

Any purchase with a total value above $10,000 must go through a formal tender process managed by the procurement team. The tender must be advertised for a minimum of 21 calendar days before evaluation begins.

Where a preferred supplier exists for a category of spend, that supplier must be used unless a documented exception is approved. The preferred supplier list is reviewed annually by the procurement team.

A purchase order must be raised and approved before any commitment is made to a supplier. Invoices received without a matching purchase order are held and returned to the originating department for regularisation.

Contracts with a total committed value above $50,000 may only be signed by the Chief Financial Officer or a delegate named in writing. Signature by any other party does not bind the company.
