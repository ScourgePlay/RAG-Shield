ACME Corporation — Performance Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in March and September. Each review covers objectives set at the previous cycle, demonstrated behaviours, and development priorities for the period ahead.

Performance is assessed on a five-point rating scale, where 1 indicates performance significantly below expectation and 5 indicates performance consistently exceeding expectation. Ratings of 1 or 2 trigger a formal support conversation.

All proposed ratings are reviewed by a moderation committee chaired by the Head of People before publication. The committee ensures consistency of standard across departments and may require a manager to revisit a proposed rating.

An employee who disagrees with a published rating may appeal in writing within 10 working days of receiving it. Appeals are heard by a manager outside the employee's reporting line and the outcome is final.

Where sustained underperformance is identified, a Performance Improvement Plan is opened for a period of 90 days. The plan sets measurable objectives with fortnightly check-ins and a documented outcome at conclusion.

Employees become eligible for consideration for promotion after 12 months in their current role. Exceptional cases may be brought forward by a department head with written justification to the Head of People.
